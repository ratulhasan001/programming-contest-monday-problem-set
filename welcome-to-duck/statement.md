They have decided to retrieve the full form only in lowercase and without any special characters. A special force of 10 ducks with 5 scientists are on the way to Earth. They are fully determined to succeed their mission doesn’t matter the cost or sacrifice. They are fully prepared with all kinds of weapons and armors with all necessary equipment and enough food.
